# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, patient and respectful. Many people arrive here from
a forum thread with a device that will not pair. Assume good intent, help them
along, and keep criticism focused on code and ideas, not on people.
Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer
privately via [GitHub](https://github.com/AMThie). Reports are handled
confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, issues or contributions
and block participants who violate this code of conduct, following the
enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
